About

I'm an engineering student at Harvey Mudd College, from Huntington Beach, California, and I like making things. My favorite part of any project is getting the part in my hands: modeling it in SolidWorks, printing or machining it, then fixing whatever doesn't fit yet.

For me, engineering is about making an impact. I want every project to build toward something bigger, whether that's tools and devices that make daily life easier for people, or robots that collect data to help society. That's what draws me to mechanical and biomedical engineering.

Beyond engineering

I throw discus and shot put in NCAA Division III track and field. In my first season I won the SCIAC discus title and was named SCIAC Track & Field Newcomer of the Year. Before Mudd, I captained my high school team.

I'm also interested in biomechanics, which overlaps more with throwing than you'd think, and in the stock market.

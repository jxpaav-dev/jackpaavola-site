Autonomous Underwater Vehicle

An underwater vehicle that holds its depth on its own. I populated the custom PCB with motor drive circuitry and pressure and temperature sensors, wound the propulsion motor, and programmed LabVIEW control software for manual driving and closed-loop depth stabilization. After waterproofing the electronics, we validated depth holding to 5 meters in pool trials.

2-person team · LabVIEW, soldering, motor winding, waterproofing
